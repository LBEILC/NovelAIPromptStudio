## Design Context

### Users

NovelAI creators who drag generated images into a focused workbench to recover, edit, translate, classify, and copy Prompt Tags, and optionally save images in a simple local gallery. Vibe data is recovered from image metadata for read-only copying or export, not edited locally. The product must remain equally usable on Windows and macOS.

### Brand Personality

Precise, creative, and studio-grade. The interface should feel like a polished creative tool: focused and technically capable, with responsive, tactile motion that preserves continuity and makes complex state changes feel effortless without becoming cryptic or visually noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined Lobe UI Tag workbench and image gallery with slate neutrals, a restrained blue primary color, image-first composition, and polished motion. Lobe UI is the canonical component and visual system. BeUI may inform motion choreography, state transitions, loading feedback, and higher-level compositions without replacing equivalent Lobe UI primitives or creating a parallel theme and styling system. Use the locally bundled Geist, Geist Mono, and HarmonyOS Sans SC families consistently across platforms. Preserve a compact professional layout, but never achieve density by making text microscopic. Motion may be visually expressive when it strengthens the image-first creative experience. Avoid neon sci-fi styling, pervasive glassmorphism, generic dashboard cards, effects without informational or experiential purpose, and one-sided accent strips for persistent selection states.

### Design Principles

1. Readability before density: primary working text is comfortable at normal viewing distance; captions are never smaller than 0.6875rem (11px).
2. Preserve the artwork as the visual focus while keeping prompt controls easy to scan and operate.
3. Use a small semantic type scale with consistent roles, weights, line heights, and tabular numerals for parameters.
4. Respect platform conventions for menus, title bars, shortcuts, paths, dialogs, and secure storage.
5. Use purposeful motion to explain navigation, loading, selection, reordering, and content changes. Frequent interactions should feel immediate; motion must remain responsive, interruptible, performance-conscious, and accessible, with complete reduced-motion and motion-off states.
6. Keep Lobe UI responsible for component semantics, interaction behavior, theming, focus, and accessibility. When drawing from BeUI, adapt its motion and state-composition patterns around existing Lobe UI or domain-specific surfaces instead of replacing equivalent primitives or importing a second visual system.
7. Express persistent selection with a complete tinted surface or full border. Reserve directional indicators for transient drag-and-drop placement only.
